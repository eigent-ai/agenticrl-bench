# Enterprise Licensing

This document explains the enterprise licensing model, activation process, and compliance requirements.

## License Types

Enterprise licenses are available in three tiers: Standard (up to 100 users), Professional (up to 1,000 users), and Unlimited. All tiers include priority support and quarterly business reviews.

## Activation

License keys are delivered via the customer portal. Enter the key in the admin panel under Settings > License to activate. Offline activation is available for air-gapped environments.

## Compliance

License usage is tracked via anonymous telemetry. Usage reports can be generated from the admin panel for internal auditing purposes.
