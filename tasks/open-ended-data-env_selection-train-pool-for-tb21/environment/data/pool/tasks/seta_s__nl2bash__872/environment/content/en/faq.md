# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I reset my password?

Navigate to the account settings page and click "Reset Password." You will receive an email with a reset link that expires after 24 hours.

## What browsers are supported?

We support the latest two major versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Internet Explorer is not supported.

## How do I contact support?

You can reach our support team through the help center or by emailing support@example.com. Response times are typically within one business day.
