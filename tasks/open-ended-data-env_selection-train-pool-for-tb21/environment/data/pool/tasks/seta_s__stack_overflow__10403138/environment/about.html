About WebProject

WebProject is a demonstration website built to showcase proper HTML accessibility practices and ARIA role usage.

Our Mission

We aim to make the web accessible to everyone by providing clear examples and best practices for ARIA role implementation.

Our Team

Our team of accessibility experts works to ensure all web content meets WAI-ARIA 1.2 standards.
